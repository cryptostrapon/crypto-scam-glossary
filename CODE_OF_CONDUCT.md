# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be useful and calm for everyone who cares about crypto scam awareness.

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Criticise facts, sources and wording, not people.
- Keep discussion on topic: scam patterns, red flags, accuracy and teaching.
- Back factual claims with a public source where you can.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Spam, promotion of products, tokens or services, and referral links.
- Naming or accusing private individuals or companies of fraud without a public source.
- Posting private data, seed phrases, keys or wallet recovery offers.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or delete comments and issues, lock threads, and block accounts that break these rules, without prior warning. Reports: cryptostrapon@proton.me.

This policy is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
